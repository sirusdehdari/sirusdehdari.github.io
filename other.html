Other

A collection of resources and links that I find useful or think others might benefit from.

Swedish Research Grants for Social Scientists

A curated overview of Swedish research grants that social scientists can apply for — from travel grants and conference organization grants to postdoctoral and project grants. Covers major funding bodies such as Vetenskapsrådet, Riksbankens Jubileumsfond, Forte, and many others.

Academic Conferences for Social Scientists

A curated overview of well-established academic conferences in political science, economics, sociology, economic history, and political economy. Filterable by discipline, continent, and who can attend — with typical timing and links to each conference website.
