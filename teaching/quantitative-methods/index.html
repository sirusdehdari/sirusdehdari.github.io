Department of Political Science · Stockholm University

Quantitative Methods

Bachelor-level instruction in quantitative research methods for political scientists, covering descriptive statistics, statistical inference, and bivariate and multiple regression analysis. Lecture slides and written assignments are available in Swedish and English below. Readings and exam information remain on Canvas.

Lecture Slides

Svenska

Interaktiva föreläsningsslides. Öppnas i webbläsaren.

Föreläsning 1 — Statistiska metoder

Datastruktur, beskrivande statistik, sannolikhetsfördelningar och statistisk inferens.

Föreläsning 2 — Regressionsanalys

Bivariat regressionsmodell, minsta-kvadratmetoden, modellens passform och statistisk signifikans.

Föreläsning 3 — Multipel regressionsanalys

Multipla regressionsmodeller, störfaktorer, kausala samband och tolkning av koefficienter.

English

Interactive lecture slides. Open in your browser.

Lecture 1 — Statistical Methods

Data structure, descriptive statistics, probability distributions, and statistical inference.

Lecture 2 — Regression Analysis

Bivariate regression, ordinary least squares, model fit, and statistical significance.

Lecture 3 — Multiple Regression Analysis

Multiple regression models, confounders, causal inference, and interpreting coefficients.
